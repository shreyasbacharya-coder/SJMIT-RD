# **App Name**: SJMIT Research Portal

## Core Features:

- Centralized Equipment & Service Listing: Display a comprehensive catalog of all research equipment and services available across departments, with detailed views including name, department, research area, description, contact information, and imagery.
- Search & Filter Functionality: Enable users to efficiently locate specific equipment or services using search queries and filter listings by department for focused exploration.
- Departmental Overviews: Showcase dedicated pages for each of the six specified departments (CSE, EEE, ECE, AIML, CE, ME), providing a gateway to their respective available research facilities.
- Admin Resource Management: Provide a streamlined Admin Panel with forms to add new equipment and services, including entry of details, department assignment, and image uploads directly to Firebase Storage.
- Dynamic Homepage Statistics: Feature an interactive hero section on the homepage displaying real-time key statistics such as Total Departments, Total Equipment, and Total Services, sourced from Firebase Firestore.
- Firebase Data & Asset Integration: Implement robust integration with Firebase Firestore for structured data persistence (equipment, services, departments) and Firebase Storage for efficient management of all image assets.
- AI-Powered Description Tool: Integrate a generative AI tool within the Admin Panel to assist administrators in crafting clear, concise, and professional descriptions for new equipment or services based on minimal input.

## Style Guidelines:

- A refined light color scheme emphasizes professionalism and readability, creating a focused academic environment.
- Primary color: A commanding academic blue (#285EAB) is chosen to convey institutional authority and trustworthiness, used for primary elements and branding.
- Background color: A very light, faintly tinted blue (#F1F5FA) provides a clean, unobtrusive canvas, enhancing content visibility without starkness.
- Accent color: A distinguished academic gold (#DBA41B) serves as an accent, strategically used to highlight crucial calls-to-action and important information, ensuring clear differentiation.
- Headline font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif), selected for its contemporary appeal and legibility, suitable for the academic tone of the portal.
- Body text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), chosen for its exceptional readability and neutral aesthetic, ensuring clear communication of all detailed information.
- Professional, minimalist line icons will be used for departments and navigational elements, aligning with the clean academic user interface.
- The design will feature a modern, responsive navigation bar, structured professional cards for displaying resources, and a consistent footer, all optimized for seamless viewing across devices.
- Subtle, non-distracting animations will be employed for smooth transitions, dynamic content loading (via skeleton loaders), and user interactions, enhancing the overall user experience.
